How it began

A love letter to Khmer food.

Sovann means “gold” in Khmer. For us, the real treasure is the time we share around a table. Our menu takes inspiration from home cooking, local markets and Cambodia’s rich food culture.

Every dish is prepared to feel familiar yet special — a way for Cambodians to reconnect with home and for visitors to discover something new.
